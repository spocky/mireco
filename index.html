    Typing

    How text from the keyboard panel is sent.

    Raise it if the TV drops letters. 80 ms works for most devices.

    If typed letters don't appear on your TV, try another format. Find the one that works with the hidden key tester (tap "Type on TV" 8 times). The Android formats add a delete key; capitals need the TV to honour Shift.
